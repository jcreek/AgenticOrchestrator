Agentic Orchestrator — install and first launch

Use a build matching your Mac: aarch64 is Apple Silicon; x64/x86_64 is Intel.
The architecture is included in the DMG filename.
Claude Code (claude) and Git must be installed and available on PATH.

1. Drag Agentic Orchestrator.app onto the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. Eject the disk image, then open Agentic Orchestrator from Applications.
3. If macOS blocks opening the app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Scroll to the message about Agentic Orchestrator and click Open Anyway.
   Confirm the prompt (and authenticate if requested), then click Open.
   If Open Anyway is missing, try opening the installed app again first.

The app has an ad-hoc bundle signature, but no Developer ID signature or Apple
notarisation. Only choose
Open Anyway for a copy you trust. Managed Macs may restrict this override.
No paid Apple Developer account is required to use this installation workflow.
Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes.

Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445

Upgrading: quit the app first (this stops its terminals), then replace the app
in Applications. Your saved sessions and settings are outside the app bundle.
